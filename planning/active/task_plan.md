# Task: stac-dem.Rmd ships pre-0.5.0 figures and could not be re-baked; its numbers do not reproduce even under the old units (#51)

`vignettes/stac-dem.Rmd` publishes numeric output produced by the units defect fixed in #49, and it
could not be regenerated as part of that work. It ships a caveat block instead (added in 0.5.0), but
the caveat is a stopgap — the figures on the pkgdown site are still wrong.

It is the **baked** half of the `.Rmd.orig` pattern: its chunks are plain ` ```r ` blocks with `#>`
output already embedded, so `R CMD check` and the pkgdown build render them as-is. Re-baking needs
the STAC endpoint reachable **and** a 1 m lidar re-run, which is why it was deferred rather than done.

## Context

`vignettes/stac-dem.Rmd` is the baked half of a `.Rmd.orig` pre-knit. It publishes cell counts and
areas from before the 0.5.0 units fix (#49), and those numbers don't reproduce even under the old
units. Exploration found two more defects on the live site (gh-pages @ 7a416c1):

- **Both figures 404.** The baked page references `figure/plot-compare-1.png` and
  `figure/site-compare-1.png`, but those files were never committed.
- **The cross-reference shows as literal text:** "Figures @ref(fig:plot-compare)". The pre-knit
  removes the chunk `fig.cap`s that bookdown needs to number figures.

The STAC endpoint is reachable (HTTP 200) and gdalcubes 0.7.5 and rstac 1.0.1 are installed, so a
re-run can be done today.

**Approved approach: live + cached** (the pars-floodplain pattern). Everything that needs no
network runs live. The STAC and 1 m work runs once in a `data-raw/` script, which caches small
outputs. A guard detects when the cache no longer matches the live algorithm.

Cache design: every downstream use of the 5 m and 1 m valley rasters resamples them to a 10 m grid
first (`valleys_stac_10`, `valleys_1m_on_10m`). So the cache can hold those small 10 m-grid rasters
plus scalar counts at native resolution, not the 2 M-cell or 14 M-cell rasters.

`gdalcubes` stays in Suggests with its `Remotes:` pin, because data-raw dependencies go in Suggests
(DBI and fresh already do this for `wsg_vignette_data.R`).
